**3D Viewer is free. You never need to pay to use it: every feature works without a supporter key.**

If you want to support it, please make a donation of **$5**, or more or less if you like. Every donation, big or small, helps keep 3D Viewer growing. Thank you! 🙏

## What the key does

Once a day, each device shows a short splash screen (10 seconds) that invites a donation, and a small semi-transparent reminder stays in the bottom-right corner. A supporter key turns both off for everyone using that server.

- Keys never expire.
- They are checked offline, without contacting any server.
- One key covers the whole server, with all its accounts.

## Get a key

- Donate with PayPal or a card at **[3dview.ittechshop.com](https://3dview.ittechshop.com)**, or
- **[buy me a coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/vincentflagg)** and write **3D Viewer** in its message. The key is emailed to the address you use on Buy Me a Coffee.

## Add it

Paste the key in **Admin → Support & ideas → Supporter license**. It is kept in the [settings backup](Backups).
