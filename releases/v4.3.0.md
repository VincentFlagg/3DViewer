**3D Viewer 4.3.0** adds **painting**, **hollowing** and **arranging on the plate** to Prepare for print.

## New

### Paint
Under **Prepare for print → Paint** in the viewer:
- Up to **8 filament colours** (the first is the model's own colour; the small square in a swatch's corner changes its colour, and the colours are remembered).
- **Brush**: paints where you drag, in the size you choose. The left button or one finger paints; the right button or two fingers turn the view.
- **Fill area**: fills a whole area, up to sharp edges (at the angle you choose) and other colours.
- **By height**: paints everything above or below a height.
- **Undo** goes back one step. **Refine mesh** splits large triangles, so painted borders follow the brush on simple models.
- **Tools → Export** saves the colours as:
  - a 3MF for **Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer**,
  - a 3MF for **PrusaSlicer**,
  - a **Coloured 3MF** for other slicers,
  - or **One part per colour**: the model cut into closed parts, one per colour, that fill it exactly and stay in place, to give each part its filament in any slicer (one object of coloured parts in a 3MF, or a .zip of STL files). The borders follow the paint to within a few tenths of a millimetre.

### Hollow
Under **Prepare for print → Hollow**, for resin printing:
- A **Wall** of 0.8–10 mm (2 mm by default). The wall thickness is approximate, within a few tenths of a millimetre.
- **Drain holes** of 1–10 mm, at the lowest points, and **Add drain holes** to drill more where you click.
- An optional **Inner lattice**, for strength (it uses more resin).
- It shows how much resin the model takes before and after.

### Arrange
- **Prepare for print → Arrange**: copies of the open model (or of its parts, after Split) packed on the plate of the printer chosen in Scale to fit, with the spacing you choose.
- **Arrange…** in the gallery's selection bar: several models, with the number of copies of each, on one plate.
- **Tools → Export** saves them in their places in a 3MF.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.3.0` (or `:4.3`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
