**3D Viewer 2.1.6** adds *Fit to screen* and *Actual size* to the folder picture's full-size preview.

## New

- **Fit to screen** (the default): every picture fills the screen. Small pictures and model thumbnails are enlarged, and big ones are shrunk.
- **Actual size** shows the picture at 100 %, starting where the frame is. Scroll, or drag beside the frame, to look around; dragging the frame and its corners works as before.
- Switch between them with the button at the top right, next to the **X**. The choice stays while you step through the pictures with the arrows.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.1.6` (or `:2.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
