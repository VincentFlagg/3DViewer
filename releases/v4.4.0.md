**3D Viewer 4.4.0** adds **cutting along a seam** and **saved paintings**, and makes colour parts much lighter.

## New

### Cut along a seam
Under **Prepare for print → Split**, choose **Along a seam** instead of **Straight cut**:
- **Click a sharp edge or a groove**: the seam follows it around the model by itself.
- Where there is no clear edge, **click along the line of the cut**, point by point. Click the first point (in red) again, or **Close the seam**, to close it: it goes on around the model in the same plane.
- **Undo point** takes back the last click; **Start again** clears the seam.
- **Cut along the seam** cuts the model along that line, with a smooth surface across the inside (flat when the seam is flat).
- The parts are joined with the same **round pegs**, **keyed pegs** or **dowel holes** as a straight cut, with the same gap. All pegs point one way, so the parts slide together.
- The parts show apart and export like any split parts: one 3MF, or one file per part.

Good to know:
- A seam has to go **all the way around** a part. One seam makes two parts; cut again for more.
- Where a smooth cut would pass through a hollow or outside the model, each side keeps what is nearest to it instead. That cut is uneven inside and has no pegs; the result says so.
- Where the cut face is too small for a peg, the parts come without one.

### Save painting
- In **Paint**, the admin has **Save painting**: the painting is kept with the model and shows for everyone who opens it, ready to export.
- It follows the model when it is moved or restored from the trash. **Remove saved painting** forgets it, and it is dropped by itself if the model file changes.
- Everyone else can still paint and export; only the admin can save.

## Changed
- **One part per colour** (Export of painted models) makes files about three times lighter, about four times faster. The outside of the model is unchanged.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.4.0` (or `:4.4`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
