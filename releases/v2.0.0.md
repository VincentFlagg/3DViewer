**3D Viewer 2.0.0** adds accounts with your own favourites, tags and print log, a private mode, new formats (STEP, IGES, 3DS, COLLADA, USD) and new tools.

## New

### Accounts
- The admin adds accounts in **Admin → Accounts** (user name and password; **New password** and **Remove** later).
- People sign in on the main page with the **person button** at the top. Accounts can look at and download models; only the admin can change the library. Everyone can change their own password from the person menu.
- The admin signs in there too, as **admin** with the admin password.
- **Private server**: tick **Admin → Accounts → Private** and only signed-in accounts and the admin see the libraries.

### Favourites, tags and print log (each account has its own)
- In the viewer, under **Mine**:
  - **☆ Favourite**: a star shows on the card;
  - **tags**: type one and press Enter; your earlier tags are suggested;
  - **Print log**: **+ Add a print** with the date, filament or resin, notes and a photo.
- The sidebar lists **My favourites**, **My prints** and each of your tags, across all libraries. Click a model there to open it in its folder.
- **Show** above the grid can narrow a folder to your favourites, printed models or a tag.
- Your marks follow a model when it is moved or its folder is renamed in 3D Viewer.

### Gallery and viewer
- **Recently added** (sidebar, for everyone): the newest models of all libraries.
- **Compare**: select two models (Select) and click **Compare** in the bar at the bottom to see them side by side, on one floor, at the same scale.
- **Print bed**: Tools → **Print bed** shows the build volume of your printer around the model (Bambu Lab A1 mini, A1, P1S/X1C, H2D, Prusa MINI+, MK4S, CORE One, XL, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic, Voron, resin printers, or a custom size) and tells you whether it fits, fits after a quarter turn, or how much too big it is.

### Formats
- **STEP (.step, .stp) and IGES (.iges, .igs)** CAD files, with the colours stored in them. Big ones get a compressed preview so they open fast after the first time. STEP files can be opened in the slicer too.
- **3DS**, **COLLADA (.dae)** and **USD (.usdz, .usd, .usda, .usdc)**, with their materials and textures.

### Admin
- **Scan schedule** (Thumbnails & previews): look through the libraries for new files every 1, 3, 6 (default) or 12 hours, or **once a day at a set time**, for example at night. The status shows the next scan.
- **Duplicates**: **Find duplicates** lists models stored more than once, in any folder or library (the same contents, whatever their names), with **Keep only this one** and **Delete** (to the trash).
- **Backup**: **Download backup** saves libraries, network shares, settings, the admin password, the accounts with their favourites, tags and print logs, and the supporter key in one file; **Restore from a backup…** puts them back, for example on a new NAS. Models, notes and thumbnails are not in it.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept, and the admin stays signed in. Nothing changes for visitors until you add accounts or make the server private.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.0.0` (or `:2.0`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
