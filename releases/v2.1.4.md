**3D Viewer 2.1.4** adds a full-size preview to the folder picture dialog.

## New

- **Folder picture** full-size preview. After choosing a picture or model thumbnail (or uploading a picture), click **View full size** or the preview itself. The whole picture fills the screen, with a bright frame on the 4:3 part the folder card shows.
- Close it with the **X** at the top right, Esc or a click beside the picture, or save it straight away with **Use this picture**.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.1.4` (or `:2.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
