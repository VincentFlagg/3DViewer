The first public release of **3D Viewer**: browse, preview and organise the 3D models on your NAS or server, from any device.

## Highlights

- **Libraries** on any volume, USB drive or network share (SMB/NFS), with a folder tree, series of related models and live search.
- **Fast thumbnails and previews** rendered in the background, on the GPU when available; large models load as compressed previews.
- **3D viewer** for STL, 3MF, OBJ, PLY, GLB/GLTF and FBX: orbit, zoom, wireframe, dimensions, and a saved orientation for models that lie on their side (the file is never changed).
- **Organise**: upload models, pictures and .zip files (unpacked into a folder), drag models and folders to move them, choose folder and series pictures, and read or write notes (README.md / .txt).
- **Themes**: Auto, Classic light and dark, Mint, Lavender, Peach, Sky and Dusk.
- **Admin page**: libraries, network shares, renderer status and cache, viewer defaults, access and password.

## Install

Download [`docker-compose.yml`](https://github.com/VincentFlagg/3DViewer/blob/main/docker-compose.yml) (also attached below) and deploy it in Dockhand, or run `docker compose up -d`. See the [README](https://github.com/VincentFlagg/3DViewer#install) for all options.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.0.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
