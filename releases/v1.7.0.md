**3D Viewer 1.7.0** adds a sun lamp to the viewer, lets you choose the angle of a model's thumbnail, and unpacks several zips in a row.

## New

- **Sun lamp.** In the viewer panel, **Light → Sun lamp** replaces the even lighting with one strong light, like the sun:
  - it casts shadows on the model and on the floor, which brings out the shape and fine details;
  - **Front**, **Left**, **Right**, **Back** and **Top** move it, and **Around** and **Height** set any direction;
  - the setting is remembered in your browser. It only changes the viewer, not the thumbnails.
- **Use this view for the thumbnail** (admin). Turn the view with the mouse (or a finger) to the angle you like, then click **Use this view** under **Thumbnail view** in the viewer panel:
  - the thumbnail (and a folder card that shows it) is rendered again from that angle;
  - the model also opens from that angle;
  - **Front view** goes back to the standard view. The model file is not changed.
- **Unpack several zips.** Select zip cards (or **Select all**) and click **Unpack** in the selection bar. They are unpacked one after another, with progress, and each zip goes to the trash.

## Fixed

- **One zip at a time.** Clicking **Unpack** twice, or two admins at once, could unpack the same zip twice. Now only one zip is unpacked at a time: a second request waits for the first to finish (and **Admin → Unpack zips** too).
- In select mode, the tick box of a card was hidden behind the card's picture.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.7.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
