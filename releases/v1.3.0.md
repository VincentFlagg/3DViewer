**3D Viewer 1.3.0** lets you keep thumbnails and previews on another disk, and opens Lychee scenes from older Lychee versions.

## New

- **`CACHE_DIR`**: thumbnails and compressed previews can live outside the data folder, for example on a bigger disk or a share. Add a volume and the variable to `docker-compose.yml`:

```yaml
    environment:
      - CACHE_DIR=/cache
    volumes:
      - /volume2/docker/3dviewer/data:/app/data
      - /volume3/3dviewer-cache:/cache
```

- At the next start the thumbnails and previews already made are moved to the new folder (the log shows how many), so nothing has to be rendered again. Settings stay in the data folder.
- **Admin → Thumbnails and previews → Stored in** shows the folder in use and its size. The start-up checklist warns when `CACHE_DIR` is not on a volume, because the cache would be lost when the container is recreated.

Details in the README under [Thumbnail and preview cache](https://github.com/VincentFlagg/3DViewer#thumbnail-and-preview-cache).

## Fixed

- **Lychee scenes from older Lychee versions** (3.5.1 and before) now open, with their models turned and placed as in Lychee and their supports, instead of failing with "byte length of Uint32Array should be a multiple of 4".

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept. Without `CACHE_DIR`, nothing changes.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.3.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
