**3D Viewer 1.7.1** renders thumbnails with the sun lamp's light and shadows.

## New

- **Thumbnails with the sun.** In the viewer, turn on **Light → Sun lamp**, place it (**Front**, **Left**, **Right**, **Back**, **Top**, or **Around** and **Height**), turn the view to the angle you like, and click **Use this view**:
  - the thumbnail is rendered from that angle with the sun's light and shadows (the shadow falls on a see-through floor, so it fits every theme);
  - with the sun lamp off, **Use this view** saves only the angle, with the usual even light;
  - **Front view** goes back to the front view with even light.

## Fixed

- When a model's orientation, view or sun was saved while its thumbnail was being rendered, the thumbnail could stay with the old setting. It is now rendered again with the new one.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.7.1` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
