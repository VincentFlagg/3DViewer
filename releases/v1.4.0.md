**3D Viewer 1.4.0** lets you select several models to download, move or delete, moves files between libraries, adds a trash, and opens G-code.

## New

- **Select several models.** Click **Select** above the grid (or Ctrl/Cmd+click a card). Click to pick, Shift+click for a range, **Select all** for the folder; a series card picks all its models. The bar at the bottom shows how many are selected and their size.
- **Download as one .zip** (for everyone). The selected files come in one zip with their sub-folders; OBJ and GLTF models bring the files they need (`.mtl`, textures, `.bin`). The zip is streamed, so multi-GB selections work. A single plain file downloads as it is.
- **Move between libraries** (admin). Drag cards onto another library in the sidebar (the libraries show up while you drag), or use **Move to…** on a folder card, in the viewer or in the selection bar to pick a library and folder. On the same disk a move is instant; to another disk or share it is copied and then deleted, with a progress bar. Notes, saved orientation and thumbnails go along. If the name is taken you are asked first, and the replaced file goes to the trash.
- **Trash** (admin). Deleting a file or a folder, now also a folder with files in it, moves it to a hidden `.3dviewer-trash` folder in its library. **Admin → Trash** restores items where they were or deletes them for good. Items older than 30 days are deleted automatically (Admin setting; 0 keeps them until you empty the trash).
- **G-code and Prusa binary G-code (.gcode, .bgcode).** See the toolpaths of a sliced print, coloured by feature (walls, infill, top/bottom, bridges, supports, skirt) with a legend. Drag the **Layer** slider to see the print layer by layer, show **Travel** moves, and read print time, filament, layer height and slicer in the panel. Thumbnails use the preview picture PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio or Cura (with a thumbnail plugin) save in the file.

## Deleting for good

The trash protects you from mistakes. Deleting from the trash (or emptying it) cannot be undone:
- the files are gone; the NAS recycle bin does not catch files deleted by 3D Viewer;
- a folder goes with everything inside it, with its notes, saved orientations, folder picture, thumbnails and previews;
- other users, apps and slicers that use the files will not find them, also on network shares.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.4.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
