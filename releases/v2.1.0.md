**3D Viewer 2.1.0** unpacks RAR, 7z, TAR and compressed files, not only zips, also with a password.

## New

- **More kinds of archives.** Everything that worked for `.zip` now works for:
  - **RAR** (`.rar`, RAR4 and RAR5), also split into parts: `Name.part1.rar`, `Name.part2.rar`… or `Name.rar`, `Name.r00`, `Name.r01`…;
  - **7-Zip** (`.7z`), also split: `Name.7z.001`, `Name.7z.002`…;
  - **TAR**: `.tar`, `.tar.gz`/`.tgz`, `.tar.xz`/`.txz`, `.tar.bz2`/`.tbz2`;
  - **a single compressed file** such as `model.stl.gz` (or `.xz`, `.bz2`): it is unpacked next to it as `model.stl`.

  That means: unpacked on **upload**, a card with **Unpack** when it was copied into a library (with its type, the number of models inside, and how many parts a split archive has), **Unpack** in the selection bar for several at once, and **Admin → Libraries → Unpack archives** for a whole library. After unpacking, the archive (every part of a split one) goes to the trash.
- **Archives with a password** (RAR, 7z and zip): **Unpack** asks for the password and tries again if it is wrong. The password is not kept. On upload and with **Unpack archives**, such archives are kept for you to unpack from their card.
- Split archives uploaded in one go are kept, and unpacked from their card once all parts are there.
- For safety, archives with links or with paths outside their folder are not unpacked, and the size limit (**Admin → Limits → Largest unpacked archive**) applies to every kind.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. The image now includes 7-Zip (p7zip with RAR support). The start-up checklist shows **Archives** when it is ready.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.1.0` (or `:2.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
