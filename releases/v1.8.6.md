**3D Viewer 1.8.6** fixes a full cache folder and adds tools to the viewer and the gallery.

## Fixed

- **Cache folder full.** When the disk or share used as `CACHE_DIR` runs out of space or reaches its quota (the log showed *Unknown system error -122* for every file, and thumbnails failed with *Upload failed (500)*):
  - moving the cache stops at the first file that does not fit, with one clear line in the log;
  - the thumbnails and previews that were not moved stay in the data folder and are still used from there; they are moved at a later start once there is room;
  - while the cache is full, new thumbnails are saved in the data folder and no new previews are made (the viewer opens the original file);
  - a thumbnail is saved even when its preview cannot be;
  - the start-up checklist shows the free space of the cache folder and warns when it is (almost) full.

## New

- **Measure.** Viewer panel → Tools → **Measure**, then click two points on the model: the distance shows on the model and in the panel, with its width, depth and height parts, in mm or inches.
- **Cross-section.** Tools → **Cross-section** cuts through the model across its **Height**, **Width** or **Depth**. A slider sets where; **Keep the other side** flips it. The cut face shows in red, so wall thickness and hollow parts are easy to see.
- **Screenshot.** Tools → **Screenshot** saves the view as a picture: **WebP** (smallest file, the default), **JPG** or **PNG** (best quality). The choice is remembered.
- **Open in slicer.** Next to Download: **Open in Bambu Studio**, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer or UltiMaker Cura (pick it in the list, it is remembered) for STL, 3MF, OBJ and PLY files. The slicer must be installed on your computer and able to reach the server at the address in your browser.
- **Sort by.** Order a folder by name (A–Z, Z–A), date (newest or oldest first), size or type. Remembered in your browser.
- **Show.** Narrow a folder to folders only, models only, models without a thumbnail, or one file type (only STL, only G-code…), with how many of each.
- **Admin → Storage.** How much space the thumbnails, previews and each library's trash take, the free space of the cache folder, the data folder and every library, and a warning when one is almost full.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept. If your cache folder was full, give it more room before restarting so the rest of the previews can be moved; Admin → Storage shows what is still in the data folder.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.8.6` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
