**3D Viewer 1.1.0** adds folder uploads, faster thumbnail rendering, an app log, and a second way to support the project.

## New

- **Upload whole folders**: drag folders onto the page, or use **Upload → Upload a folder…**. Subfolders are kept, and big uploads go in parts with one progress bar.
- **Thumbnails rendered at the same time**: choose 1 to 4 in **Admin → Thumbnails & previews**. More helps with a GPU, many CPU cores or libraries on network shares.
- **Folder pictures sized for the card**: pictures are cropped to 4:3 and scaled down to 800 × 600 before saving. For the sharpest result, upload 800 × 600 pictures (4:3).
- **App log**: every start shows the 3D Viewer logo and a checklist (web server, data folder, libraries, network shares, renderer, uploads, admin, license). After that, one dated line per event, with the reason for every error. Open it with **Logs** in Dockhand, Container Manager or Container Station, or `docker logs 3dviewer`.
- **Buy Me a Coffee**: [buymeacoffee.com/vincentflagg](https://buymeacoffee.com/vincentflagg). Write **3D Viewer** in the coffee message and your supporter key is emailed to you.
- **Textured OBJ models** show their textures in thumbnails, previews and the viewer.

## Changed

- The daily splash shows for 10 seconds, and a small reminder stays in the corner until a supporter key is activated.
- Node 24 LTS base image, rebuilt weekly with the latest security fixes.

## Fixed

- Dropping a folder failed with "Upload failed (network error)".
- Uploads longer than 5 minutes were cut off.
- An OBJ model's texture picture was used as its cover, and some textured models showed black.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.1.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
