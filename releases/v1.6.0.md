**3D Viewer 1.6.0** renames folders, creates folders while moving, and shows what each folder holds.

## New

- **Rename folders** (admin). Use the pencil button on a folder card, or **Rename** above the grid for the folder you are in.
  - The models inside keep their notes, saved orientation, thumbnails and previews: nothing is rendered again.
  - If you are inside the folder (or one of its sub-folders), the page follows it to its new name.
  - A name that is already taken is refused; changing only upper/lower case works too.
- **New folder in Move to…** In the **Move to…** picker, **+ New folder** creates a folder where you are (in any library) and opens it, so **Move here** moves into it. The new folder is kept if you then cancel the move.
- **Folder cards show what they hold**, sub-folders included, for example **12 folders · 10 models · 35 files**:
  - "files" are everything that is not a model (images, notes, zips, slicer files…); hidden files and the trash are not counted;
  - the counts are worked out in the background and remembered, so big libraries still open fast; until they arrive, a card shows what is directly in it;
  - they are checked again after every change made in the app, and every few minutes for changes made outside it (over SMB, for example).

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.6.0` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
