**3D Viewer 4.0.0** is the biggest update yet: it sends prints to your printers, keeps a shared To print list, speaks Spanish, and adds two-factor sign-in, single sign-on, notifications, statistics and more.

## New

### Printing
- **Send to printer**: sliced files (G-code, `.bgcode`, a sliced 3MF) go straight from the viewer or the To print list to a printer on your network, and can start right away. Each printer shows whether it is free or printing.
  - **Klipper** via Moonraker (Mainsail, Fluidd), and **Creality** K1/K2/Hi/Ender-3 V3 with Moonraker reachable
  - **OctoPrint** (API key)
  - **Prusa** MK4, XL, MINI, Core One via **PrusaLink**
  - **Bambu Lab** X1, P1, A1 in **LAN mode** (send a 3MF saved with “Export plate sliced file”)
  
  Add printers in **Admin → Printers** (with **Test connection**) and tick **Can print** for the accounts that may use them.
- **To print**: a shared list of what to print next, with copies, material and a note. Reorder it, tick off printed copies (they go into your print log), and send from it.
- **3MF plates and colours**: projects from Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer show their plates (one at a time or all) and their filament colours.

### Library
- **Similar models**: the viewer lists models from the whole library that look like the open one (copies, versions, remixes), by shape, whatever their names, size or position.
- **Edit many models at once**: select models or folders and set the series, description, licence and your tags in one go.
- **Licence** field for each model (for example CC BY-NC 4.0).
- **Statistics** (signed-in accounts): models and space, added, opened and printed per month, by type and by library, the largest and the most used models.

### Staying informed
- **Notifications** when new models turn up, an import finishes, someone opens a share link or a backup fails: **Telegram**, **Discord**, **e-mail**, **ntfy** and **Gotify** (Admin → Notifications).
- **API tokens** (read-only or full access) for scripts and **Home Assistant**: `GET /api/stats/summary` with `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. The README has a ready-made sensor.

### Sign-in and safety
- **Two-factor sign-in** with any authenticator app, with recovery codes. Optional for everyone; the admin can require it.
- **Single sign-on** with your OpenID Connect provider (Authelia, Authentik, Keycloak, Zitadel, Pocket ID…). Passwords keep working; accounts are made on first sign-in.
- **Automatic backups** of the settings into a folder every day or week, keeping as many as you choose (Admin → Backup).

### Languages
- 3D Viewer now speaks **English** and **Spanish** (Español). It follows your browser; the globe button at the top changes it.
- **Help translate**: corrections and new languages are welcome as [translation issues](https://github.com/VincentFlagg/3DViewer/issues/new?template=translation.yml). See *Languages and translations* in the README.

## Faster and lighter

- The 3D view only draws while something moves (turning, zooming, a tool), and uses no CPU or GPU while you just look at it: easier on laptops and phones.
- Card pictures load as you scroll to them, not all at once: big folders open faster and use less memory and network.
- Thumbnails drawn in your browser give their graphics memory back when done.
- On the server, the thumbnail renderer replaces its pages every 50 models to free memory, and starts Chromium without background services it does not need.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.0.0` (or `:4.0`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
