**3D Viewer 2.1.7** can fit the whole picture in a folder card, with black or white borders.

## New

- **Fill or Fit whole picture**, in the Folder picture dialog and in its full-size preview:
  - **Fill** (the default) crops the picture to the 4:3 folder card. You choose the part with the frame, as before.
  - **Fit whole picture** keeps all of the picture, shrunk to fit the card. The empty sides (left and right, or top and bottom) are filled with **Black** or **White** borders.
- The small preview and the full-size preview show the card exactly as it will be saved.
- Each picture keeps its own choice while you step through them with the arrows.
- GIFs keep Fill, because they are saved as they are, still animated.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.1.7` (or `:2.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
