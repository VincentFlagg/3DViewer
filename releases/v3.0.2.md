**3D Viewer 3.0.2** shows what is inside the folders the search finds.

## Changed

- **Folders in search results** show their folder picture (a `cover` image or the model chosen as the folder thumbnail).
- **Without a folder picture** they show a 2×2 collage of model thumbnails: the folder's own models first, then models from its sub-folders. Models without a thumbnail yet are left out.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:3.0.2` (or `:3.0`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
