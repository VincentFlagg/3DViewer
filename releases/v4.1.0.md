**3D Viewer 4.1.0** adds **Prepare for print** to the viewer: tools that get a model ready for the slicer, right in the browser.

## New

Open a model and look under **Prepare for print** in the viewer panel:

- **Best orientation**: 3D Viewer tries the model on each side and on its flat faces and shows the **three best positions**, each with:
  - its supports, in grams,
  - its height,
  - a rough print time.

  Click one to turn the model; the faces that still need supports turn red. Admins keep a position with **Save orientation**.
- **Scale to fit**: pick your printer and see how big the model can be, with 5 % of the build volume to spare (turned on the bed when that fits better). One click sets that scale for the export.
- **Simplify**: keep 10–90 % of the triangles (50 % by default) for a lighter file that slices faster. It says how far the surface moved at most.
- **Add text**: type a name or a label, pick a font, the letter height and the depth, choose **Raised** or **Engraved**, and click the model where it goes. It follows curved faces and reads upright from where you look.

Everyone can use these tools and download the result with **Tools → Export** (STL, 3MF or OBJ). The admin can also save it as a copy next to the model. The model file itself is never changed, and **Undo all changes** goes back to it.

Times and weights are estimates without slicing: your slicer has the final word.

## Fixed

- After an update, the gallery could stay blank in a browser that had used 3D Viewer before, until its cache was cleared.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.1.0` (or `:4.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
