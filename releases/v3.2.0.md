**3D Viewer 3.2.0** can be installed as an app on your computer, phone or tablet.

## New

- **Install app** (the phone icon at the top of the page): 3D Viewer gets its own icon and opens in its own window, without the browser's bars.
  - **Chrome, Edge and Android** install it from an **https://** address. Over plain http, the button explains the options: HTTPS through your NAS's reverse proxy, Tailscale or a tunnel, or Chrome's *Create shortcut → Open as window* on a computer.
  - **iPhone and iPad**: Share → **Add to Home Screen** (works over http too); the button shows the steps.
- **Two app icons**: a shaded **3D** cube (the default) or a flat **2D** one. Choose in **Admin → Install as an app**. The browser tab, the top-left logo and the supporter screen use it too.
- When the server cannot be reached, the installed app shows a short message instead of the browser's error page.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:3.2.0` (or `:3.2`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
