**3D Viewer 2.1.5** lets you step through the pictures and choose the part that is saved in the folder picture's full-size preview.

## New

- **Arrows** in the full-size preview step to the previous or next picture or model thumbnail in the folder. The **←** and **→** keys and a swipe on phones do the same, and the top bar shows where you are (for example *3 of 7*).
- **Editable frame.** Drag the white frame to choose the part of the picture that becomes the folder picture, and drag its corners to make it bigger or smaller. It always stays 4:3, like the folder card. **Reset frame** puts it back in the middle.
- Each picture keeps its frame while you step through them, and the small preview in the dialog shows your frame. **Use this picture** saves exactly that part.
- GIFs keep a fixed frame, because they are saved as they are, still animated.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.1.5` (or `:2.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
