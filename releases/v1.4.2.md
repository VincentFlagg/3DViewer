**3D Viewer 1.4.2** labels the dimensions in the viewer.

## Changed

- **Dimensions now say which is which**: **W** (width, left to right) × **D** (depth, front to back) × **H** (height, bottom to top), measured as the model stands in the viewer, for example `W 23 × D 34 × H 34 mm`. They follow the model when you turn it (Tilt, Turn, Roll) or switch Z-up, and the height is always the last number.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.4.2` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
