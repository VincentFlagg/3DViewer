**3D Viewer 2.0.1** makes measuring exact and adds Creality Print.

## New

- **Snapping for Measure.** Under **Tools → Measure**, **Snap to** chooses where each point goes:
  - **Auto** (default): a corner close by, else the middle of a border, else the border itself;
  - **Corners**: where borders meet (the join points);
  - **Borders**: the nearest sharp edge;
  - **Middle of a border**: the middle of the whole straight edge;
  - **Centre of a flat face**: the middle of the flat face you click;
  - **Centre of the model** and **Centre of the model's base**;
  - **Nothing**: exactly where you click.

  A ring follows the pointer and shows where a click will land (also just outside the model's outline, to catch its outside edges). The panel says what each point snapped to. The choice is remembered and can be changed between the first and second click, for example from a corner to the centre of the model.
- **Open in Creality Print.** Creality Print 6 (and later) is in the **Open in** list next to Download, for STL, 3MF, OBJ, PLY and STEP files.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:2.0.1` (or `:2.0`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
