**3D Viewer 1.4.3** lists ZBrush files in the gallery.

## New

- **ZBrush files (.ztl, .zpr)** now appear in the gallery, with a ZBrush icon, next to the models of their folder. ZBrush's formats are not published, so they cannot be shown in 3D or get a thumbnail, but you can:
  - click one to download it;
  - select them with models to download as one .zip, **Move to…** or **Delete**;
  - drag them to another folder or library.
- A model moved to another folder takes a ZBrush file with the same name (`dragon.stl` and `dragon.ztl`) along.

Tip: for a preview, export the model from ZBrush as STL or OBJ (Export, or the 3D Print Hub plugin) and keep it next to the `.ztl`.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.4.3` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
