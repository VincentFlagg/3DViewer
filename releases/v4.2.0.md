**3D Viewer 4.2.0** adds **supports** and **splitting models into parts with pegs** to Prepare for print.

## New

### Supports
Under **Prepare for print → Supports** in the viewer:
- **Tree** supports (branches that join into trunks: less plastic, fewer marks) or **Straight** columns.
- **Touch points** from 0.4 to 1.0 mm, and **Sparse**, **Normal** or **Dense** spacing, at the overhang angle of Tools → Overhangs.
- It shows how much plastic the supports take and how many of them stand on the model.
- **Tools → Export** saves them with the model as their own part (in a 3MF), or as a file of their own.

Turn the model first (Best orientation helps): supports are made for the model as it stands. Your slicer can still make its own instead; these are to check, and for any printer or slicer.

### Split
Under **Prepare for print → Split**:
- **Cut here**: across the height, width or depth, wherever you drag the cut (or type it in mm).
- **Cut to fit the printer**: as few cuts as make every part fit the printer chosen in Scale to fit.
- The parts are joined with:
  - **Round pegs**,
  - **Keyed pegs**, with a flat side, so they fit one way only,
  - **Dowel holes**: holes in both parts, plus loose dowels to print (or use metal pins or magnets),
  - or **No pegs**.
- The **Gap** around the pegs is 0.1–0.4 mm (0.2 mm by default).
- The parts show apart, each in its own colour. **Tools → Export** saves them as one 3MF with each part separate, or **One file per part** in a .zip.

Splitting needs a closed model; Export's repair runs first.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.2.0` (or `:4.2`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
