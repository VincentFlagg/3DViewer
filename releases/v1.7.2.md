**3D Viewer 1.7.2** keeps the folder header in view while you scroll.

## Changed

- The folder title, its tools (**Select**, **Picture**, **Rename**, **Add info**, card size, **Grid**/**List**) and the upload/unzip progress bar now stay pinned under the top bar while you scroll, so you can follow an upload or switch tools from anywhere in a long folder. On very short screens (a phone on its side) the header scrolls away as before, to leave room for the models.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.7.2` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
