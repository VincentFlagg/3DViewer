**3D Viewer 1.4.4** lets you choose whether ZBrush files show in the gallery.

## New

- **Admin → Viewer defaults → Show ZBrush files (.ztl, .zpr) in the gallery.** On by default, as in 1.4.3. Untick it to hide them: the files stay in their folders and are not changed, they just don't appear as cards. Tick it again to show them.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.4.4` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
