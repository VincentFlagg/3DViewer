**3D Viewer 3.0.0** adds print checks to the viewer: wall thickness, overhangs, a mesh check, and weight and cost. Measuring and the cross-section get new tools too.

## New

### Wall thickness
Tools → **Wall thickness** colours the whole model by how thick its walls are.
- Red is below **Thin below** (0.8 mm by default). Thicker walls go orange, yellow, green and then blue.
- The thinnest spots are labelled on the model with their thickness.
- It runs in the background, with progress, on models of up to 6 million triangles.

### Overhangs
Tools → **Overhangs** shows in red the faces that will need supports.
- The **Angle** slider is the slicer's overhang threshold, 45° by default.
- It says how much of the surface needs supports. Faces on the bed don't count.

### Mesh check
Tools → **Mesh check** finds and highlights:
- open edges (holes), in orange;
- non-manifold edges, in purple;
- flipped faces, in red.

It counts each kind and the separate parts. A hollow inside a part is recognised as such, not as flipped.

### Weight & cost
Tools → **Weight & cost** shows the volume, plastic used, weight, filament length and cost.
- **Materials:** PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, TPU, Nylon, PC, PLA-CF and resin.
- **Settings:** infill, number of walls, and 1.75 or 2.85 mm filament.
- **Prices:** the admin sets the currency and the price per kg of each material in **Admin → Print cost**. Each person can change a price for themselves in the viewer.
- **Accuracy:** it is an estimate without slicing, usually within 10–15 % of a slicer, without supports.

### Measure
- **Distance** now shows its ΔX, ΔY and ΔZ parts.
- **Circle:** click three points on the edge of a hole or a round part to get the radius, diameter and centre.
- **Angle:** click three points to get the angle at the middle one.

### Cross-section
- Type the exact cut height, or the distance from the left side or front.
- The cut face is hatched. This can be turned off with **Hatched cut faces**.

## Fixed

- The cross-section's red cut face could be hidden behind the model's own inside faces. It shows again.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:3.0.0` (or `:3.0`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
