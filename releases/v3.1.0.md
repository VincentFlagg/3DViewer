**3D Viewer 3.1.0** adds export with repair, print orientation, AR, share links, import from a link and a better compare.

## New

### In the viewer
- **Export** (Tools): download the model as **STL**, **3MF** or **OBJ**, standing on the bed as shown.
  - **Scale** in %, or a **Height** in mm.
  - **Repair** (on by default): closes holes, turns flipped faces the right way, and removes faces with no area or stored twice. It then says what it fixed and what is left.
  - Admins can **Save a copy here**, next to the original (for example `benchy-fixed.3mf`). The original is never changed.
- **Lay flat on a face** (Orientation): click a face and the model turns to lie on it.
- **Auto-orient for printing** (Orientation): finds the position that needs the fewest supports, then the biggest footprint. Save it with **Save orientation**.
- **View in AR** on phones and tablets: the model at its real size, on your table or floor.
  - iPhone and iPad use AR Quick Look.
  - Android uses Google's Scene Viewer. The phone must reach the server at its address, and Scene Viewer may need HTTPS.
- **Compare with…**: pick a second model from the folder or by searching the library.
- **Overlay** in Compare: the two models on top of each other, each in its own see-through colour, to see what differs.

### Sharing and importing
- **Share links**: **Share link** in the viewer, or **Share** above a folder.
  - Choose how long it works: 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or until removed.
  - Whoever opens it sees only that model, or that folder and its sub-folders, and can view and download. This also works on a private server, without an account.
  - Remove links in the dialog, or all of them in **Admin → Share links**.
- **Import from link**: **Import** at the top of the page, for signed-in accounts and the admin.
  - **Printables**, **Thingiverse** or **MakerWorld** model page: a new folder with the files, the pictures (the first one becomes the folder picture) and a README with the description.
  - **Thingiverse** needs a free app token: add it in **Admin → Import from link**.
  - **MakerWorld** only lets its members download files, so usually only the pictures and description come in. Download the 3MF there and upload it.
  - **A link to a file** (.stl, .3mf, .zip…): saved in the open folder; archives are unpacked.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:3.1.0` (or `:3.1`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
