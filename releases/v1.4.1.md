**3D Viewer 1.4.1** makes G-code files look like the printed part.

## Changed

- **Solid, shaded toolpaths.** G-code was drawn as thin flat lines, so the colours of walls, infill and supports blended together and the part had no definition. Every printed line is now a shaded 3D strand with its real width and layer height: walls, layer lines and the top surface show clearly, like an STL. Thumbnails of G-code without a slicer picture use the same look.
- **New controls in the viewer panel** (G-code only):
  - **Solid** / **Lines**: lines are lighter for very large files, which open as lines.
  - **By feature** / **One colour**: one colour shows the print in the model colour.
  - A tick box per feature in the legend: untick infill and inner walls to see only the outside. Every feature is shown when a file opens.
- Solid/Lines and the colour choice are remembered in your browser.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser. Your libraries, settings, notes and thumbnails are kept. To get the new look in G-code thumbnails that were already rendered without a slicer picture, use **Admin → Thumbnails and previews → Clear thumbnails**.

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:1.4.1` (or `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
