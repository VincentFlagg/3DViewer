**3D Viewer 4.5.0** adds **Place parts**: lay cut parts flat, turn them and move them on the plate.

## New

### Place parts
Under **Prepare for print → Place parts**, after **Split** (or **Arrange**):
- **Click a part** to choose it (it lights up). **Drag** the chosen part to move it on the plate; drag anywhere else to turn the view as usual.
- **Lay flat**: the part turns onto its largest flat face that can rest on the plate, which is usually its cut face.
- **Lay on a face…**: click the face of the part that should go down.
- **Tip forwards**, **Tip sideways** and **Quarter turn**: quarter turns in each direction. **Spin** turns it on the plate by any angle.
- **Lay all parts flat**: every part at once, then packed side by side on the printer chosen in Scale to fit.
- **Pack on the plate**: packs the parts side by side as they lie now.
- **Tools → Export** saves the parts as you placed them (3MF keeps their places), or one file per part.

Good to know:
- A part whose only flat face has a peg sticking out of it cannot lie on that face, so it is left as it is; the result says how many. Turn it yourself, or print it with supports.
- If the parts do not all fit the printer, they are laid side by side anyway and the result says so.
- **Undo all changes** goes back to the model as it was.

## Update

Pull the new image and redeploy (or `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`), then hard-refresh your browser (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).

Image: `ghcr.io/vincentflagg/3dviewer:4.5.0` (or `:4.5`, `:latest`), for amd64 and arm64.
